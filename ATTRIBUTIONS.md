## Why These Repositories Exist

The system prompts of commercial AI products represent valuable
intellectual property and engineering effort. They are typically kept
confidential to:
- Prevent competitors from copying prompt engineering techniques
- Avoid exposing internal product strategies
- Protect against adversarial attacks that exploit known prompt patterns

However, researchers, security experts, and the open-source community
have argued that transparency benefits:
- Helps developers understand how AI tools actually work
- Enables security research on prompt injection attacks
- Promotes learning and innovation in prompt engineering
- Provides accountability for AI behavior

The creators of the source repositories believe that **AI systems
transparency benefits everyone**, and that the public has a right to
understand the instructions shaping the AI tools they use daily.

## Ethical Use

This tool is designed for:
- ✅ Learning prompt engineering
- ✅ Auditing your own AI products
- ✅ Security research on prompt injection
- ✅ Building better AI agents

**Not for:**
- ❌ Bypassing safety measures of commercial AI products you don't own
- ❌ Creating adversarial attacks on production systems
- ❌ Violating the Terms of Service of any AI provider
- ❌ Generating harmful content

Use responsibly. The maintainers of this tool are not responsible for
misuse of the indexed prompts.
